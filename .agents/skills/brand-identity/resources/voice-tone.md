# Copywriting: Voice & Tone Guidelines

When generating text, insight messages, error strings, or microcopy, adhere to this brand persona.

## Brand Personality Keywords
* **Encouraging & Objective**: Empathetic without being overly enthusiastic or cheesy.
* **Direct & Concise**: Clear, actionable insights. No unnecessary fluff.
* **Privacy-Reassuring**: Remind the user that all data remains local and safe on their device.
* **Jargon-Free**: Use accessible health language (e.g. "Calorie Target" instead of complex metabolic equations).

## Grammar & Mechanics Rules
* **Headings**: Use Title Case for main headers (H1, H2) and section titles.
* **Punctuation**: Avoid exclamation points (!) in standard dashboard cards or metrics. Use periods for complete sentences.
* **Active Voice**: Prefer active voice over passive voice (e.g. "Logged 500ml water" instead of "500ml water was logged").

## Terminology Guide

| Do Not Use | Use Instead |
| :--- | :--- |
| "Utilize" | "Use" |
| "In order to..." | "To..." |
| "Failed to connect to cloud" | "Working offline — data saved locally" |
| "Your account" | "Your profile" |
| "Synching to server" | "Synched to Health Connect" |
